About Edward

Portrait © Charlie Kirkpatrick

Edward Tait is a British composer, pianist and organist whose work brings together a commitment to new music and a wide-ranging engagement with the concert repertoire.

His compositions have been performed by ensembles including the London Sinfonietta, Carducci Quartet and Orion Orchestra. His work includes collaborations with performers and ensembles, and projects as both a composer and pianist.

He is the founder and artistic director of the MetroLand Music Festival and runs the publishing venture NoteWelder Music. His musical work encompasses solo and chamber performance, composition, organ playing and education.
